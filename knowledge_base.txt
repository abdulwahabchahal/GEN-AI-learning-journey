## Admissions
Undergraduate applications for the fall semester open on March 1 and close on June 30. Applicants must submit a high school transcript, two recommendation letters, and a personal statement of up to 500 words. Admission decisions are emailed within three weeks of the deadline.

## Tuition and Fees
Undergraduate tuition at Northfield University is $4,500 per semester. There is a one-time registration fee of $150, and science and engineering students pay an additional lab fee of $200 per semester. Tuition can be paid in two installments, and the second installment is due by the end of week six.

## Scholarships
Merit scholarships cover up to 75% of tuition for students with a GPA above 3.8. Need-based grants cover up to 50% of tuition, and applications for them close on July 15. Scholarship holders must maintain a GPA of at least 3.0 to keep their award.

## Housing
On-campus hostels offer shared rooms at $300 per month and single rooms at $480 per month, with meals included. Housing applications must be submitted by August 1. The hostels are a 10-minute walk from the main campus, and the gates close at 11 PM.

## Library
The central library is open from 8 AM to midnight during exams and 8 AM to 8 PM otherwise. Students can borrow up to 8 books for 21 days, and the late fee is $0.50 per day per book. A 24-hour study room is available in Block C.

## Grading and Probation
A student must score at least 50% in every course to pass. A GPA below 2.0 leads to academic probation, and students on probation must meet their academic advisor every month. A failed course can be retaken once.

## Attendance and Exams
Students must attend at least 75% of classes to be allowed to sit the final exams. Makeup exams are available only with a medical certificate submitted within 5 days of the missed exam.

## Careers and Internships
The career office arranges summer internships with partner companies for third-year students. Resume workshops are held every Thursday, and the annual career fair takes place in October.

## Health and Counseling
Free confidential counseling sessions are available for all students by appointment. The health clinic is open from 9 AM to 5 PM on weekdays, and a 24-hour emergency line is available at extension 911.

## IT Services
Students connect to the campus Wi-Fi network called Northfield-Student using their student ID. Every student receives a free university email account and 50 GB of cloud storage. Laptops can be borrowed from the IT desk for up to 14 days.

## Transport
University buses run from five city locations to the campus every 30 minutes on weekdays. Students who drive need a parking permit, which costs $60 per semester.
